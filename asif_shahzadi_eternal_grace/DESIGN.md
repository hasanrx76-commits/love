## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an intimate, poetic, and couture celebration of love. Conceived for "Asif & Shahzadi", it merges romantic editorial grandeur with sleek modern digital craft. The emotional response is deeply atmospheric, graceful, and warm—evoking the sensation of bespoke stationary, heirloom jewels, and candlelit twilight.

The aesthetic philosophy draws upon **Romantic Glassmorphism** layered over an editorial haute-couture foundation:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Frosted pearl panels, luminous liquid-champagne borders, and rose quartz specular highlights.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-fashion serif typography punctuated by refined, whisper-quiet sans-serif body text.
- **Jeweled Accents:** Precise, fine-line gilded ornaments, metallic gold rim light, and deep wine focal points that ground ethereal blush hues.
- **Sensory Modernity:** Soft, tactile hover states, seamless physics, and unhurried luxury that honors memory milestones, time countdowns, and poetic literature.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around romantic velvet, antique golds, and soft morning light:

- **Primary (`#8A2846` - Royal Rose Wine):** A deeply saturated, romantic burgundy that commands visual weight for primary calls-to-action, key typography highlights, monogram seals, and anchor moments.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Vintage Champagne Gold):** A luminous, warm metallic gold applied to delicate divider lines, countdown digits, shimmering border highlights, and decorative badges.
- **Tertiary (`#DDA7A5` - Rose Dust Blush):** A tender, poetic mid-tone rose used for subtle interactive fills, secondary tags, pill overlays, and glow reflections.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7` - Alabaster Pearl):** A bespoke, warm ivory canvas that replaces stark white, providing warmth and an heirloom paper finish.

### Functional Surface Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#FDFBF7` (Alabaster Pearl)
- **Glass Surface Base:** `rgba(255, 252, 249, 0.72)` with backdrop filter blur
- **Subtle Surface Tint:** `#F7EFE9` (Warm Cashmere)
- **Deep Contrast Typography:** `#2D181C` (Espresso Wine)
- **Secondary Subtitle Ink:** `#694F56` (Muted Mauve Slate)
- **Glowing Auroras:** Radial gradients shifting between `#DDA7A522` and `#D4AF371A`

## Typography

The pairing creates an immediate romantic tension between classical editorial grandeur and crisp contemporary clarity.

- **Playfair Display (Serif):** Deployed for monumental declarations, names, countdown labels, and milestone headers. It captures editorial fashion cues with distinct high-contrast serifs, sweeping ligatures, and expressive italics.
- **Manrope (Sans-serif):** Crafted for effortless readability in micro-details, event logistics, RSVP forms, narrative paragraphs, and metadata. Its geometric structure balance Playfair’s ornamentation.
- **Typographic Rules:** Never set `Playfair Display` in tight blocks of small text. Ensure `label-caps` always features expanded tracking (0.15em - 0.25em) to maintain an elevated, gallery-grade presentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column symmetrical fluid grid with generous vertical cadence, instilling a sense of breath, timeless pacing, and calm.

### Grid Rhythm & Fluid Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (16px), minimum outer page margin of 20px. Content flows in a linear, tactile sequence with edge-to-edge card margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 36px margins. Cards cluster in 2-column arrays.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter-desktop` (32px), with a constrained centered container at `container-max` (1200px). Sections alternate asymmetrical narrative layouts (e.g., 5-column story text paired with 7-column romantic imagery).

### Spacing Philosophy
Ample vertical padding (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`) ensures that milestone markers, countdown clocks, and photo moments never feel crowded. Negative space acts as a quiet luxury framing device.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent layered light and champagne warmth rather than heavy gray shadows:

- **Soft Glassmorphism:** Glass card surfaces use `background: rgba(255, 252, 249, 0.75)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
- **Luminous Rim Borders:** Rather than dark borders, cards and modal tiers are defined by ultra-fine dual borders: an outer hairline border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)` and an inner specular stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`.
- **Rose-Tinted Ambient Shadows:** Elevations employ warm, diffused cast shadows tinted with primary wine and blush undertones:
  - *Tier 1 (Cards & Chips):* `0 4px 24px -2px rgba(138, 40, 70, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`
  - *Tier 2 (Modals & Floating Nav):* `0 16px 40px -4px rgba(138, 40, 70, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8) inset`
  - *Tier 3 (Hero Highlights & Medallions):* `0 20px 60px -8px rgba(138, 40, 70, 0.18), 0 0 30px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`
- **Atmospheric Glows:** Underneath key countdown nodes and milestone markers, diffuse radial light pools (`#DDA7A5` at 15% opacity, 120px blur radius) project from behind elements to simulate soft candlelight.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (`2`)** shape discipline with targeted pill treatments:
- **Base Components (Cards, Inputs, Dialogs):** Configured with an 8px (`0.5rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) radius, providing gentle curved corners that soften the screen without appearing juvenile.
- **Interactive Buttons, Tags, and Pill Badges:** Configured with complete capsule pills (`9999px`), imparting an organic, polished pebble feel.
- **Editorial Frames:** Image frames and photo highlights incorporate arched headers (`border-top-left-radius: 9999px; border-top-right-radius: 9999px`) to echo classical cathedral arches and antique portraiture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Couture Wine):** Fill of `#8A2846` with `#FDFBF7` text. Pill-shaped. Subtle gold border highlight (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`). Hover state initiates a soft golden bloom glow and smooth micro-scale (`scale(1.02)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Background of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Bordered in `#D4AF37` at 50% opacity. Text in `#8A2846`.
- **Text / Poetic Links:** High-tracking uppercase serif or italic link with an expanding gold underline animation on hover.

### Romantic Badges & Chips
- Capsule-shaped chips rendered in `#DDA7A5` at 18% opacity with a fine `#D4AF37` stroke. Text set in `label-caps` using `#8A2846`. Used for date announcements, schedule tags (e.g., "MEHNDI", "RECEPTION"), and dress code cues.

### Glass Cards & Memory Milestones
- **Glass Story Cards:** Multi-layered panels with frosted pearl backdrops, inner glow specular borders, and soft padding (`space-xl`).
- **Milestone Timeline:** Vertical hairline in `#D4AF37` (40% opacity) centered with pearlescent timeline nodes. Milestone events display the date in gold uppercase labels, header in Playfair Display, and photography encased in arched frames.

### Countdown Clock
- Architectural digit containers featuring frosted glass surfaces, hairline gold borders, and ambient rose glows.
- Numerical values set in `Playfair Display` (`headline-lg`), paired with `label-caps` in `#8A2846` below the counter units ("DAYS", "HOURS", "MINUTES", "SECONDS").

### Love Quote Highlights
- Editorial quote callouts centered inside a pill-tinted glass container. Flanked by stylized vintage golden quotation marks. Body formatted in `quote-display` with a delicate center divider ornament beneath the verse.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Semi-translucent white backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), 1px warm border (`#DDA7A5`), transitioning to a crisp `#D4AF37` border with a subtle wine-colored aura on `:focus`.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** Circular elements with custom gold-leaf check fills and soft rose-wine backgrounds when selected.
